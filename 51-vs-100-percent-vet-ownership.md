## The Texas HUB Program No Longer Exists — It's Now VetHUB

This is the biggest Texas contracting news you haven't heard yet. Effective December 2, 2025, the Texas Comptroller restructured the HUB program under emergency rules, renaming it **Veteran Heroes United in Business (VetHUB)** and restricting eligibility exclusively to service-disabled veterans. Businesses previously certified based on race, ethnicity, or sex had their certifications revoked.

The Texas Comptroller has since completed the final step in the overhaul, formally ending all regional certification agreements. Since the relaunch, the number of certified businesses rose from 547 to 1,075 veteran-owned businesses — the highest number of service-disabled veteran participants in the program's history.

This is a remarkable shift: the program went from 15,790 certified businesses to roughly 1,075. The pool of certified competitors just shrank by 93%.

## To Your Specific Question: Does 51% vs. 100% Affect VetHUB?

**No — 51% is the threshold, and that's all you need for VetHUB.**

VetHUB certification requires proof of **51% ownership** and control of the day-to-day operation of the business. The owner must be a service-disabled veteran with a service-related disability of 20% or greater. VetHUB certification is free of charge and good for up to four years.

So the ownership breakdown between the two programs is now:

| Benefit | Ownership Required | You Qualify At |
|---|---|---|
| Texas franchise tax exemption (5 yrs) | **100% veteran** | Only if sole owner |
| Texas filing fee waiver ($300) | **100% veteran** | Only if sole owner |
| **VetHUB certification** | **51% veteran** | ✅ Yes |
| Federal SDVOSB | **51% veteran** | ✅ Yes |
| Federal VOSB / VA Veterans First | **51% veteran** | ✅ Yes |

The state contracting certification (VetHUB) and the state tax benefits (franchise exemption) have **different ownership thresholds**. You don't need 100% ownership to access Texas state contracts through VetHUB — just 51%.

## What VetHUB Actually Gets You

State agencies use the VetHUB Directory in conjunction with the Comptroller's Centralized Master Bidders List (CMBL) to solicit bids from certified VetHUBs for state purchasing and public works contracts. Prime contractors also use the VetHUB Directory to identify VetHUBs for subcontracting opportunities.

And the market is significant — in FY2025, HUB businesses secured roughly $2 billion in contract awards, representing 11% of the state's total procurement spending. With only ~1,075 certified businesses now eligible to compete for that pool (vs. 15,000+ before), your share of visibility and opportunity is dramatically higher.
